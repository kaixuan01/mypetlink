# Post 01: Lost pet? Your first 72 hours

**Goal:** a checklist people save and send to friends. The brand comes in softly on slide 7.
**Formats:** 8-slide carousel (Instagram, Threads, TikTok photo mode) + 44s Reel / TikTok.
**One call to action:** save it and share it.
**Source:** idea 1 in the [competitive brief](../../competitive-brief-2026-10.md). No competitor owns
Malaysia-specific lost-pet guidance.

## Instagram (carousel, then the Reel a few days later)

```
Lost pet? Here's what to do in the first 72 hours 🐾

We hope you never need this. But if it happens, you won't be thinking clearly, so save it now.

⏱ Hour 0–2: search close to home and tell your guard house + neighbours
📞 Hour 2–24: call your council's animal control, vets and shelters. Post a clear notice.
📌 Hour 24–72: flyers, revisit the pound, search at dawn and late at night
🛡 Before it happens: photos, microchip number and a Safety Profile anyone can scan

Save this 📌 and send it to a pet parent you love.

#petsofmalaysia #catsofmalaysia #dogsofmalaysia #lostpetmalaysia #mypetlink
```

**Alt text (slide 1):** Checklist cover reading "Lost pet? Your first 72 hours" with the
MyPetLink mascots, Linko the cat and Linky the dog.

## Threads (text, with the carousel attached)

```
If your pet goes missing in Malaysia, the first few hours matter most. A quick checklist:

1. Search close to home. Cats often hide quietly nearby, so go back after dark with a torch.
2. Tell your guard house, your neighbours and your residents' WhatsApp group.
3. Call your council's animal control early. Don't wait.
4. Post a clear notice: face photo, full-body photo, general area. Never your home address.

Full list in the pics. What would you add? 👇
```

(The question at the end invites replies, which is what Threads rewards. It isn't a second
call to action.)

  code on 2026-10-15.
