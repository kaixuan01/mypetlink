## Fact notes (check before posting)

- Smart Tag price, "one-time" and "available now": live on mypetlink.com.my/pricing on
  2026-10-15.
- The phone screen is the real Safety Profile for the Linko demo account. No real phone number
  is shown. The developer badge in the bottom corner is cropped out of `assets/safety-profile-linko-demo-top.png`.
- AirTag: a finder can tap an AirTag in Lost Mode with an NFC phone to see the owner's message
  (Apple's Lost Mode feature). The location comes from nearby Apple devices.
- "Some ID tag services charge a subscription" is true in Malaysia: Petotum puts its tag on a
  paid plan. We deliberately don't name them.
- GPS "usually a subscription": Tractive requires one. Some trackers don't, hence "usually".
