# MyPetLink Xiaohongshu — account strategy

## Positioning

**Not** "a pet app account". **Yes** "a Malaysian pet owner who is unusually organised
about their pet, and shares what they learn."

The product appears because it is genuinely part of that person's routine, the same way
a skincare account shows the bottle it actually uses. Roughly **1 in 3** posts touches
the product; the rest earn the right to.

## Voice

Written the way Chinese-Malaysians actually type on XHS: Simplified Chinese base, English
nouns left in English where nobody translates them (vaccine, WhatsApp, QR, condo, vet),
occasional Malay (jalan, kampung, rumah). Warm, practical, a little self-deprecating.
Never corporate, never "尊敬的用户".

- ✅ 「我第一次带猫去打针，紧张到手抖 😅」
- ❌ 「MyPetLink 致力于为马来西亚宠物主人提供全方位解决方案」

## Format rules

- **Cover image is 80% of the outcome.** 3:4, 1080×1440. Big readable Chinese title,
  4–8 characters, high contrast. If the cover fails, the content does not matter.
- **Title** ≤ 20 characters. Curiosity or a concrete number.
- **Carousel** 4–9 slides. Slide 1 = cover, last slide = CTA or summary.
- **Video** 1080×1920, 15–45s. Hook in the first 2 seconds, on-screen text always
  (most viewers watch muted).
- **Hashtags** 6–10, mixed: 2 broad (#养猫日常), 3 mid (#马来西亚养宠), 2 niche
  (#宠物走失), 1 branded (#MyPetLink).
- **Save-worthiness beats like-worthiness.** 收藏 drives reach on XHS. Checklists,
  templates, and cost breakdowns get saved. Design for the save.

## Posting cadence

5 posts per week, Weeks 1–6. Best local slots: **12:00–13:30** (lunch) and
**20:30–22:30** (evening scroll). Sunday evening is the strongest single slot.

| Week | Pillar mix |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Awareness ×4, Education ×1 |
| 2 | Awareness ×3, Education ×2 |
| 3 | Education ×5 |
| 4 | Education ×1, Product discovery ×4 |
| 5 | Product discovery ×2, Trust ×3 |
| 6 | Trust ×2, Conversion ×3 |
| 7 | Conversion ×1 + repost best performers |

Front-loading Awareness and Education means that by the time a Conversion post appears in
Week 6, the account has already been useful ~20 times. That is what makes the ask land.

## Pillar shape

| # | Pillar | Posts | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Awareness | 1–7 | Be findable and relatable. Zero product. |
| 2 | Education | 8–15 | Be genuinely useful. Product appears only where it truly helps. |
| 3 | Product discovery | 16–21 | Show the thing working. Still no hard sell. |
| 4 | Trust / behind-the-scenes | 22–26 | Show the humans and the limits. Say what we cannot do. |
| 5 | Conversion | 27–30 | Ask, once, clearly. |

## What we measure

| Metric | Target by end of Week 6 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 收藏率 (save rate) | > 8% on Education posts | The single best XHS reach signal |
| Profile visits per post | > 3% | Whether the cover + bio combination works |
| `/sample` sessions from XHS | 200+ | Cold curiosity, measurable in analytics |
| Free profiles created | 50 | The only number that actually matters |

Track link-outs with the existing analytics events. Do **not** add new tracking, cookie
banners, or consent tooling for this campaign.

## Standing rules

1. No GPS or tracking language, ever.
2. No "buy a tag" CTA while tags are Coming Soon.
3. No fabricated reunion stories.
4. No real phone numbers, addresses, or non-consenting faces on screen.
5. Every product claim must be true on the live site the day it posts.
