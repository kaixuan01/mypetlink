# Pillar 1 — Awareness (Posts 1–7)

**Job:** be findable and relatable to Malaysian pet owners who have never heard of us.
**Product rule:** zero product. Not one screenshot. These posts earn attention only.

Footage legend used throughout:
- 🤖 **AUTO** — MyPetLink can generate or screen-capture this without you filming
- 📱 **PHONE** — you must record it

## Post 1 — 第一次养猫的 24 小时

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Hook** | 「接猫回家的第一个晚上，我一夜没睡 😭」 |

| **Story** | Hour-by-hour of night one: cat hides under the sofa for 5 hours, refuses food, you google "kitten not eating" at 2am, then at 6am it finally comes out and sits on your foot. |

| **Format** | 📸 Carousel, 8 slides |
| **Footage** | 8 stills: (1) carrier at the door, (2) cat hiding under furniture, (3) untouched food bowl, (4) your phone showing 2am, (5) you sitting on the floor waiting, (6) first cautious step out, (7) cat on your foot, (8) cat asleep |

| 📱 PHONE | All 8. Shoot low, at cat height. Warm lamp light, no flash. |
