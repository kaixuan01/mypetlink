# Shot list

All clips: vertical 9:16, 1080p or higher, 4–6 seconds each. Hold one second before and
after the useful action.

1. `cat-watching-door.mp4` — real pet calmly watching a closed door. No staged lure.
2. `cat-checking-window.mp4` — real interest in a secure window/grille/mesh. The barrier
   must remain safely closed.
3. `cat-door-excitement.mp4` — real alert behavior when a safe internal door moves. Do not
   expose the pet to an exit.
4. `open-gate-empty.mp4` — an empty doorway/gate risk illustration filmed with the pet
   securely elsewhere. No address or house number.
5. `secure-door-check.mp4` — hands checking latch, grille, or reminder sign; reusable for
   moving-day and emergency-checklist content.

No escape, search, or reunion shot is requested because none should be fabricated.
