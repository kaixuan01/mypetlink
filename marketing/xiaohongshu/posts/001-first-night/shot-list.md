# Shot list

Shoot or select eight real vertical-friendly stills from the same pet's actual first night.

1. Carrier at the doorway; no address, keys, or private papers visible.
2. Pet hiding safely under furniture; do not force or reposition the animal.
3. Untouched food bowl.
4. A quiet waiting moment from the owner’s point of view; no notifications visible.
5. Owner sitting at floor level, face optional.
6. Pet’s first cautious step into the room.
7. First voluntary close interaction.
8. Pet resting safely.

Use warm available light, no flash, and shoot at the pet’s eye level. Do not recreate the
night later and present it as documentary footage.
