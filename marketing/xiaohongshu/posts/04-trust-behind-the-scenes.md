# Pillar 4 — Trust / behind-the-scenes (Posts 22–26)

**Job:** turn a product into people. On Xiaohongshu, a small local team that shows its
face and admits its limits outperforms a polished brand voice.
**Product rule:** the product is context, not subject. At least one post here must say
something genuinely unflattering — that is the entire point of the pillar.

## Post 24 — 为什么坚持不做 GPS

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Hook** | 「很多人问：为什么不加 GPS？」 |
| **Pain point** | The single most common objection. Answer it once, properly, in public. |
| **Story** | The honest reasoning: GPS trackers need charging, are heavy on a cat, cost RM200+ plus a monthly SIM, and are useless the moment the battery dies. A QR needs no battery and works forever. Different tool for a different job — and we say when a GPS tracker is genuinely the better choice. |
| **Feature** | Positioning, not features. |
| **Format** | 🎬 Video, 35s, face to camera |
| **Footage** | You explaining; optionally hold up a GPS collar and a printed QR tag to compare weight |
| 🤖 AUTO | None |
| 📱 PHONE | Vertical talking head. The physical comparison in hand is the strongest beat — a heavy collar next to a paper tag. |
| **On-screen text** | 0s `为什么不做 GPS？` / 7s `要充电` / 13s `猫戴着重` / 19s `RM200+ 还要月费` / 26s `没电就没用` / 31s `我们选另一条路` |
| **Caption** | Say outright: if you have a large dog that roams and you can charge it weekly, a GPS tracker may genuinely suit you better. Recommending against yourself buys enormous credibility. |
| **CTA** | 「你会选哪种？评论区聊 👇」 |
