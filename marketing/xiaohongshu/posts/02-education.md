# Pillar 2 — Education (Posts 8–15)

**Job:** be genuinely useful whether or not the viewer ever signs up.
**Product rule:** the product may appear **only** where it is the natural answer, and
never before the useful part has been delivered. Roughly half these posts have no
product in them at all.

## Post 12 — 关于宠物 QR，最大的误会

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Hook** | 「宠物 QR 不是 GPS。我说清楚一次。」 |
| **Pain point** | Owners assume a QR tag tracks their pet, then feel cheated. Or they dismiss QR entirely because they think it is inferior tracking. |
| **Story** | Corrective, confident, myth-busting. A QR does nothing until a human scans it. That is the whole point: it works with people, not satellites, and it needs no battery. |
| **Feature** | Concept only. **No tag purchase CTA — tags are Coming Soon.** |
| **Format** | 🎬 Video, 30s |
| **Footage** | Phone camera opening a Safety Profile by scanning a printed QR |
| 🤖 AUTO | ✅ The Safety Profile page as it appears after the scan. Also the downloadable QR PNG from the Share Center. |
| 📱 PHONE | The physical act: print the QR on paper, film a phone camera scanning it and the page opening. Real hands, real phone. |
| **On-screen text** | 0s `❌ 宠物 QR ≠ GPS` / 6s `它不会追踪你的猫` / 12s `它是给「捡到的人」用的` / 18s `扫一下 → 直接 WhatsApp 你` / 24s `不用电池 · 不用 app` |
| **Caption** | Be direct about the limitation. Owners trust a brand that says what it cannot do. Add: 「我们没有 GPS，也不打算假装有。」 |
