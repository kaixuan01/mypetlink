# Shot list

Film one overhead flat-lay session in natural window light.

1. Litter/sand packaging actually purchased in the measured month.
2. Food and treats actually purchased in the measured month.
3. Vet receipt from the measured month, fully redacted except date/category/amount.
4. Toys, carrier, or miscellaneous supplies actually included in the total.
5. A redacted cost worksheet or receipts that support the final total; this is evidence,
   not necessarily a publishable slide.

Do not include borrowed products or invent a “typical” basket. Cover clinic name, owner
name, address, phone, invoice identifiers, and payment details.
