# MyPetLink Xiaohongshu visual system v1

This template stays close to the product: warm cream, coral, blue, mint, rounded cards,
dark navy text, and the existing MyPetLink logo. It is intentionally simple enough to
reuse without a designer.

## Layout

- Video: 1080 x 1920, 30fps. Cover: 1080 x 1440.
- Keep important text inside 100px left/right, 220px top, and 300px bottom margins.
- Hook: two or three short lines, 4–12 Chinese characters per line, navy with one coral
  or blue accent block.
- Subtitle: white text on a 78% navy rounded rectangle, above the bottom safe margin.
- Logo: small and quiet—bottom left on covers, top right inside the safe area on video.
  It identifies the account; it is not the hook.
- CTA: one action only. Until Smart Tags launch, the product CTA is to create a free pet
  profile or view the sample. Awareness posts keep comment/save CTAs only.

## Motion and sound

- Use hard cuts by default and a 3–4% slow zoom on stills.
- Hold important UI for at least 0.75 seconds. Avoid decorative transitions.
- Voice is normalized to -16 LUFS. Optional user-supplied BGM is reduced and ducked
  under voice. No music is stored in this repository.

## Hierarchy

1. Hook: immediate reason to keep watching.
2. One short subtitle per shot.
3. Supporting label or number.
4. CTA on the final frame only.

Prototype covers use a visible `制作样片` badge. Remove that badge only when every
required real asset has passed the manifest check and the post has been manually reviewed.
