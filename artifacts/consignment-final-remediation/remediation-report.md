## Data and compatibility

The existing capability catalogue remains authoritative; no new role grants or role-name checks were added. Receipt, discrepancy and resolution retain the existing reconcile capability plus the business independence checks. The API uses existing envelopes, request validation and400/403/409 conflicts; new/reused reservation201/200 now conveys the actual outcome. No actor ID comes from a client.

`ConsignmentReceivingState` is separate from physical custody status. A partially received delivery remains Dispatched until its receiving work is complete. The legacy Received delivery state is displayed as “Receiving complete”, including when actual returns resolve the outstanding receiving work; it never asserts that returned units were received at the partner. `ReceivedQuantity` snapshots the latest actual partner receipt's physically counted subset, including damaged units; its recipient/date/reference come from that same event. Zero-count return reviews stay in history and do not overwrite the actual partner receipt. If stock never physically reached the partner, those receipt fields remain null. Current good stock is derived separately from unit classifications, never substituted for that physical count. Current partner good stock, damaged/missing findings and unconfirmed stock are displayed separately. “Open partner custody” retains outstanding custody accountability, including unlocated stock; it is not presented as a physical count of good tags on the shelf.

Initial full undamaged receipts can omit unit groups for compatibility. Partial findings and resolutions must identify affected units. Returned IDs are valid only after an actual return event; a receiving count cannot silently create a return. A shortage can remain unresolved while unrelated confirmed good stock proceeds. Permanent shortage liability, loss allocation and financial adjustment are deliberately not fabricated.

New additive EF migration **20261017160448_CompleteConsignmentReceivingAndApproval** adds contributor evidence and per-unit receiving/issue-recorder fields. Historical issued PDF bytes, commercial snapshots, unit IDs, custody and movements remain unchanged. Legacy drafts have incomplete contributor history and need a fresh version. Known historical Received/ExternalHandover facts remain good; old open unresolved receiving facts become ReviewRequired with the last recorded issue actor where available. Unknown historical recorder evidence fails closed and needs a reviewed recovery plan. No contributor or missing/good count is invented.

Current B2C catalogue/checkout may still reserve against Pending stock as intended. New wholesale/consignment claims continue protecting prior inspected retail capacity. Already-held business shipments do not reconsume shelf capacity. Wholesale document numbering, allocation-change voiding and shipment snapshots remain covered by regression tests. No automated document pack, settlement engine, payment flow or Partner Portal was added.
